Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

UKPetPassport.com (“we”, “us”, “our”) is committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR).

1. Who we are

This website is operated by the owner of UKPetPassport.com. For data enquiries, please contact us via the email address provided in any correspondence from this site.

2. What data we collect

  • Name and email address — when you submit the opt-in form to receive the free checklist
  • Usage data — if you consent to analytics cookies, Microsoft Clarity collects anonymised information about how you interact with this site (pages visited, clicks, scroll depth). No personally identifiable information is passed to Clarity.

3. How we use your data

  • To deliver the free UK Pet Passport Checklist you requested
  • To send follow-up emails with relevant pet travel information (you can unsubscribe at any time)
  • To improve the website using anonymised analytics (only with your consent)

4. Legal basis for processing

  • Consent — you give explicit consent when you submit the opt-in form and when you accept analytics cookies
  • Legitimate interests — to operate and improve the website

5. Data processors

We use the following third-party services to process your data on our behalf:

  • ClickFunnels — email list management and delivery (youradvantage.clickfunnels.com). Their privacy policy is available at clickfunnels.com/privacy.
  • Microsoft Clarity — anonymised website analytics, only loaded with your consent. Microsoft’s privacy policy: privacy.microsoft.com.
  • Cloudflare — website hosting and security. Cloudflare’s privacy policy: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

6. Cookies

We use a cookie consent banner on this site. If you accept cookies, Microsoft Clarity will set analytics cookies. If you decline, no tracking cookies are set. Your preference is stored in your browser’s local storage.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by clearing your browser’s local storage or cookies for this site.

7. Data retention

We retain your email address for as long as you are subscribed to our mailing list. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email we send. On unsubscribing, your data is removed from our email platform.

8. Your rights under UK GDPR

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict processing of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO)

To exercise any of these rights, reply to any email you have received from us or contact us directly.

9. International transfers

ClickFunnels and Microsoft are US-based services. Data transfers are covered by standard contractual clauses and UK adequacy decisions where applicable.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date at the top will always reflect the most recent version.

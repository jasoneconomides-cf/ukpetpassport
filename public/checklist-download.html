What to check before you travel

📋 First

Read the checklist

Start with the AHC timing, rabies date and microchip details. Tiny numbers matter more than anyone would like.

👩‍⚕️ Next

Book an OV appointment

Your usual vet may be able to help if they are an authorised Official Veterinarian.

🐕 Dogs

Plan the return rules

Dogs usually need tapeworm treatment before entering Great Britain, with some direct-return exemptions.

🔎 Always

Check official guidance

Rules can change. Confirm your plan with GOV.UK, your destination country, your carrier and your vet.
